The Winter of Lanterns

The kingdom of Marrowfell lies between the Kharn Mountains and the River Sable. Its capital, Ashgate, was built around the Glass Library, where the kingdom's charters are kept. For three hundred years Marrowfell was ruled by House Varn.

King Aldous Varn died in the autumn of the year 412 and left two children. His elder daughter Isolde inherited the Ember Crown and became queen; the people soon called her the Pale Queen. His younger son Cassian was named Warden of Kharn Hold, the fortress that guards the only road through the Kharn Mountains. Soldiers called him the Grey Warden.

Real power, however, sat with Maelric Voss, head of the Council of Nine. Voss, known as the Iron Chancellor, had served King Aldous for thirty years. He distrusted Isolde and thought her too soft. He found an ally in Cassian, who resented that his sister had received the crown. Voss promised the Grey Warden that he would one day wear it himself.

Isolde trusted few people. Her closest adviser was Orren Thale, the court archivist, whom the courtiers called the Quiet Scribe. What she did not know was that Orren served the Order of the Lantern, a secret society sworn to protect the kingdom's charters from any ruler who would burn them. The Order was led by Seraphine Dusk, whom its members called Mother Lantern. Seraphine had taught Orren everything he knew about the old records. She hated Voss, because twenty years earlier the Chancellor had executed her husband, the scholar Edmund Dusk, for heresy.

Orren kept the Sunless Key, which opened a vault beneath the Glass Library. In that vault lay the Charter of House Varn. The Charter declared that the crown passes to the eldest child of a king, and that any chancellor who forged a royal decree must lose his office. If it were ever read aloud before the Council, it would end Voss's power. Orren's niece, Tamsin Reed, a smuggler who sailed the River Sable, carried secret messages between the Order and the palace.

In the winter of 413 the Corsair League of the Ember Isles offered Marrowfell peace. Its leader, Admiral Brandt Halloran, proposed the Frost Treaty, to be sealed by his marriage to Queen Isolde. Isolde agreed to the match. Voss opposed it, because the Council's gold came from war taxes, and he began to plan.

On the Night of Lanterns, Voss ordered the Glass Library burned so the Charter would be destroyed. Cassian's soldiers surrounded the building. Orren used the Sunless Key, opened the vault, and gave the Charter to Tamsin through a cellar door. Cassian's captain, Dorn Hask, found Orren inside the burning library and killed him. Tamsin escaped by boat down the River Sable with the Charter hidden in a barrel. She carried it to Admiral Halloran in the Ember Isles.

Halloran sailed back up the river with the Charter and his fleet. When Isolde learned that her own brother had helped Voss, she rode to Kharn Hold and confronted him. Cassian, ashamed, surrendered the fortress. Isolde did not execute him; she exiled him to the Ember Isles.

Back in Ashgate, Halloran read the Charter aloud before the Council of Nine. Because Voss had forged decrees in the queen's name, the Council stripped the Iron Chancellor of his office. Seraphine Dusk, who had waited twenty years for this day, took Orren's place as archivist. Isolde and Halloran were married, and the Frost Treaty was sealed. The Charter now rests in the Glass Library, which was rebuilt, and Dorn Hask was never found.
